# Report tables, 21 August 2026

Three tables for `report_draft_2026_08_19_v6.md`, replacing the `[TABLE 1]`,
`[TABLE 2]` and `[TABLE 3]` placeholders in sections 3.1, 3.2 and 3.3.

Every number generated by `tables_21aug.py` and logged to
`reads/2026_08_21_tables.txt`. Eleven seed basis. Cost is `shadow_tip`,
accumulated hinged tip force in newton steps. Bootstrap intervals are 5000
resamples with `default_rng(0)`, resampling seed means for the seed level
interval and anatomy means for the anatomy level interval.

Captions are written to state findings rather than restate axes, and go into
the template caption style so numbering is automatic. Every column header
carries its unit.

## Table 3

Section 3.3, replacing the `[TABLE 3]` placeholder.

| Prediction method | Calibration runs | Error, training arch types | Error, unseen arch types |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Budget, identity, cost = d | 0 | 0.52 | 0.29 |

| Penalty, fitted, all five weights | 5 | 6.86 | 6.14 |

**Caption.** Predicting realised cost from the budget requires no calibration
and is more accurate on unseen arch types than a fit that costs five full
training runs, at 0.29 against 0.52. The fit degrades by a factor of twenty
eight when asked to extrapolate beyond the weights it was built on, which is
the operation required whenever a target force falls outside the swept range.
Mean relative error under leave one out over the five penalty weights, log cost
regressed on log weight.

## Notes carried from the generation run

Cost is `shadow_tip`. `ep_cost` is exactly zero on every eval row because
`evaluate.py` uses `ZeroCost()`, and `hinge_cost_shadow` is byte identical to
`shadow_max`, which is the device signal rather than the tip signal.

Excluded from every condition by name: `_ep30` and `run_epoch-50` checkpoint
evals, `cmp_*_stoch` stochastic robustness checks, and the canary, both
signals and probe diagnostics. `r1_s*` excluded as a duplicate of
`r1_baseline_s*`. `r4_w0.0003_s*` excluded as the earlier device signal
condition, distinct from `r4tip_w0.0003_s*`.

The penalty is monotone in realised cost on unseen arch types, reading 69.47,
40.45, 32.28, 7.07 and 0.21 as the weight ascends, and is not monotone on the
training arch types, where the second and third weights invert at 17.55 and
18.87. This now comes from source rather than from the claims sheet.
